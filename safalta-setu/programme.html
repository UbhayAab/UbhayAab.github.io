What we aim to support

Discovery, conversation
and clearer pathways.

These are intended programme outcomes. The delivery format, eligibility, timing and participation arrangements will be published after approval.

01

Career awareness

Help students explore a wider range of careers and understand the work behind them.

02

Guided mentorship

Make space for questions, reflection and conversations with people who can encourage and explain.

03

Educational clarity

Help students and families understand subject choices and the routes available ahead.
