Our intended approach

Small conversations.
Wider horizons.

Choosing subjects or thinking about a career can feel like a big decision. The programme we are developing brings three kinds of support together.

01Career discovery

Discover possibilities

Explore careers beyond the options already familiar at home and school.

02Guided mentorship

Have a conversation

Find space to ask questions, understand interests and learn from a mentor.

03Pathway clarity

Understand the pathway

Make subjects, streams and educational routes easier to discuss with family.

For families shaped by service

A steady circle of guidance,
through changing chapters.

Army life can bring moves and transitions. We aim to offer students and families a consistent space to explore educational choices with confidence and care.

Our role is to help a student understand their options and decide their next step.
